the problem

Plumbing still sells over the phone

Most plumbing shops in Egypt sell over the phone or on WhatsApp. Customers can't see what's in stock or which sizes exist. Plumbers send long lists of fittings that someone prices by hand, and orders, deliveries and cash live in a notebook.

The shop needed a store that speaks Egyptian Arabic first, takes cash when the order arrives, works with any delivery option, and is simple enough for one person to run every day.

Order to door in five steps

each step can only move to the next allowed one
  1. 1NewThe order comes in, stock is held.
  2. 2ConfirmedThe shop calls to confirm.
  3. 3PackedInvoice and packing slip printed.
  4. 4ShippedShipper and tracking link saved.
  5. 5DeliveredCash collected, sale counted.

How it works

for the customer, then for the owner
1

Find parts the way people ask for them

  • 10 categories, from mixers and toilets to pipes, fittings and tools.
  • Size search the way people say it: نص بوصة, 1/2 inch and 20 مم all find the same items.
  • Filters for price, size, finish (chrome, matte black, brushed gold), sale items and in stock only.
2

From product page to the door

  • Photo gallery with zoom, and a size and finish picker that shows stock for each option.
  • Bag and checkout on one page: discount codes, an address that fills in from the last order, and a landmark field, because that's how people give directions here.
  • A 5-step tracker with a notification at each step, including phone push notifications.
  • Cancel before packing, rate after delivery, chat with support, or send a WhatsApp list.
3

One panel for the owner

  • Orders board with a tab per status and one-click next-step buttons. Every move is logged with who did it and when.
  • Stock that stays right: taken when the order is placed, returned on cancel or return.
  • Products with ready-made size sets for pipes (mm and inch), heaters (litres) and basins (cm).
  • Money reports by delivered date, exported to PDF and Excel, with Arabic that prints correctly.
  • Discount codes, shipping fee, invites, invoices, packing slips, support inbox and backups.
4

Phone first

Most orders come from phones. A bottom bar keeps Home, Categories, Orders, Bag and Account within thumb reach, the header shrinks as you scroll, and the WhatsApp button is always one tap away.

5

Its own design

I made a design system just for this store, so it looks nothing like the marketplace it came from.

  • Deep water teal for navigation, burnished copper only for buying actions, so the eye always finds the next step.
  • Navy bands with a faint pipe-grid pattern for the hero, footer and admin menu.
  • Flat cards with thin borders and 4px corners, like a neat parts counter.
  • IBM Plex Sans in Arabic and English, with equal-width numbers so prices line up.
  • Light, dark and follow-my-phone modes. A logo of a pipe elbow with a copper drop.
