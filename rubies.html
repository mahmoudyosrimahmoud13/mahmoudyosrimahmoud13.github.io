the problem

Great shops, scattered across the city

Alexandria has lots of small boutiques with good pieces, but buying from three of them means three deliveries, three fees, and paying up front for clothes that might not fit. Most of them sell through Instagram messages with no real store behind them.

Rubies gives each shop its own storefront, puts every shop in one bag, sends one courier to collect it all, and lets the customer try the pieces at the door and pay cash only for what they keep.

Four apps in one

one for every person in the chain
for

Customers

  • Each shop has its own page and logo.
  • Search every shop with filters for size, colour, price and rating.
  • The bag groups items by shop, with one delivery fee.
  • Live tracking with a QR handover pass.
for

Shop owners

  • An order queue: accept, pack, mark ready.
  • Catalog and stock, with low-stock warnings.
  • Sales, best sellers, busiest days and hours.
  • What the shop is owed, and payouts.
for

Couriers

  • The next stop, with Directions and the bags to pick up.
  • Pick up, deliver and returns in three tabs.
  • Cash on hand, and a QR to hand it to the admin.
  • Arabic and dark mode for night shifts.
for

Admins

  • Orders that need a courier, cash with couriers, returns to shops.
  • Financial report with PDF export.
  • Money and payouts per shop.
  • People, shops, windows, fees, announcements and join requests.
and

Arabic first, light and dark

Arabic is the default, with a full right-to-left layout. English is one tap away, and every screen works in light and dark.
